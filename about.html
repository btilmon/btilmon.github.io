About

I work on high-fidelity geometry, lighting, and material reconstruction for objects and scenes where complex light transport makes conventional photogrammetry and neural rendering difficult.

I am currently at Quidient. Before that, I completed my PhD in the FOCUS Lab at the University of Florida, advised by Sanjeev Koppal. I have collaborated with Uland Wong, Shuohchen Su, Sizhuo Ma, and Jian Wang.
