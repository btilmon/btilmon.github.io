apple-log2linear

apple-log2linear is a custom implementation of Apple ProRes Log decoding for calibrated iPhone video.

The project keeps the decode step explicit, which is useful when phone video needs to enter a reconstruction, inverse rendering, or radiance field pipeline as linear image data instead of display-ready footage.
